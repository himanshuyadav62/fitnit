# Training template research note

Reviewed: 6 September 2026

## Product decisions

- The app offers both full-body and split routines because a 2024 systematic review found similar strength and hypertrophy outcomes when weekly volume was equated. Schedule fit and adherence should decide the split.
- Each major muscle group receives repeat weekly exposure in the general and hypertrophy templates. This is a practical way to distribute useful weekly volume, not a claim that frequency itself is uniquely anabolic.
- Strength templates place heavier multi-joint lifts first, use lower rep ranges, and allow 2.5–3.5 minutes of rest. Hypertrophy accessories mostly use moderate-to-high rep ranges and 75–120 seconds of rest.
- Most working sets stop with about 2–3 repetitions in reserve. Training to momentary failure is not required for strength or muscle gain and can make recovery harder.
- The five-day plan separates a low-impact conditioning session from heavy lower-body strength work. The six-day plan is labeled intermediate and keeps the second deadlift exposure deliberately low volume.
- The vegan starter targets approximately 1.6–2.0 g protein/kg/day. Evidence suggests benefits level off around 1.6 g/kg/day on average, while protein-matched plant-based diets can support comparable adaptations in young adults.

## Built-in library

| Days | Template | Intended user | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Minimalist Full Body | Beginner, limited schedule | Two full-body sessions |
| 3 | Beginner Vegan Muscle Gain | Beginner, hypertrophy | Three rotating full-body sessions |
| 3 | Strength Foundations | Beginner, strength skill | Squat/bench, deadlift/press, volume practice |
| 4 | Upper / Lower Builder | Intermediate, hypertrophy | Two upper and two lower sessions |
| 5 | Strength + Athletic | Intermediate, all-round fitness | Four lifting days plus conditioning/core |
| 6 | Push / Pull / Legs | Intermediate, hypertrophy | Two push, pull, and leg exposures |

These templates are starting points for healthy adults. Pain, medical restrictions, pregnancy, recent surgery, or unexplained cardiovascular symptoms require individualized professional guidance.
